Privacy Policy

Short version: QuadFix collects nothing.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the QuadFix app for iPhone, iPad and Mac ("the app") and this website, both provided by BitCraft Solutions ("we", "us").

The app

  • No data collected. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and makes no network connections.
  • Your photos stay on your device. QuadFix runs inside Apple's Photos app. Corner detection and image processing happen entirely on your device. We never see your photos.
  • Saved corners. Your corner positions are stored with the edited photo by Photos itself, so you can readjust them later. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs them along with your photos under Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to them.
  • Photo access. QuadFix only sees the photo you choose to edit, and only while you edit it. It can't browse your library.

This website

The website sets no cookies and uses no tracking scripts or client-side identifiers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. As with any website, these providers briefly process your IP address to deliver pages and for basic security and abuse protection.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is built to work without cookies or persistent identifiers. It gives us aggregate, non-identifying counts such as page views, approximate country, referring site, and browser or device type. It can't recognise you individually or follow you across other websites.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we receive what you choose to send, such as your email address, and use it only to reply. We don't share it with anyone. Ask us any time to delete it.

Children

QuadFix is suitable for everyone and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions? Email contact@bitcraftsolutions.com.
