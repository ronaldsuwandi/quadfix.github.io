Where is QuadFix?

QuadFix lives inside the Photos app, not on its own.

  • iPhone and iPad: open a photo, tap Edit, tap •••, then Extensions → QuadFix.
  • Mac: double-click a photo, click Edit, click ••• and choose QuadFix. If it's not listed, turn it on in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → Photos Editing.

Readjusting and undoing

  • To adjust the corners later, open QuadFix again on the same photo. You'll see the original with your corners.
  • To undo only QuadFix's correction, open QuadFix, tap Remove Correction and save.
  • Photos' Revert to Original always undoes everything.

Why can't I readjust my corners?

Photos only keeps the newest edit's details, so QuadFix can readjust a correction only when it was the photo's first edit:

  • Edited in Photos before QuadFix (crop, filters, adjustments): the correction can't be readjusted or removed later. QuadFix tells you so and starts fresh on the edited photo.
  • Edited in Photos after QuadFix (for example a rotation): reopening QuadFix starts fresh on the rotated result.

In both cases, use Revert to Original to start over, or duplicate the photo first. Best of all: use QuadFix first, then your other edits.

Known limitations

  • Auto-detect works best on paper against a contrasting background. Check the corners on busy photos.
  • Pressing Cancel can freeze Photos for about 10 seconds. This is a Photos issue that affects every editing extension, including Apple's Markup.
  • No rotation inside QuadFix: use Photos' own rotate tool.
  • Still photos only: no video or Live Photo support.
  • No keyboard control on iPad.
